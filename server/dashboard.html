Settings

HP and lives apply at the next reset.

Rules

  • Blue P1-P4 vs Red P5-P8: an enemy hit = -1 HP. +10 pts per hit, +50 per kill.
  • First Blood: first hit of the match gets +25.
  • Finisher: whoever takes the last life gets +100.
  • Teammates are Medics: their shot heals +1 HP (+5 pts), then a medic cooldown.
  • Friendly fire: shooting while your medic is on cooldown = a strike (-10 pts).
  • Traitor: too many strikes and that player defects. Their shots now do damage.
  • Spawn shield: after respawning, shots are blocked for a few seconds.
  • Shooting: one shot per second. After taking a hit, you can't shoot for 10 seconds.
  • Hit cooldown: after taking damage, enemy shots are ignored briefly.
  • Eliminate the other team, or have more lives left when time runs out.
